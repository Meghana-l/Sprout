## What it is

In early education, teachers jot quick shorthand all day — "Mia, nap 12:30–2, ate all her peas, cried at pickup." At the end of the day that shorthand has to become the update families actually read, for every child. Turning a room full of scribbles into warm, readable messages is slow, repetitive work that eats an hour or more, every day.

Sprout does that rewrite. Paste the notes you already jot, and it writes a finished, ready-to-send update for each child — kind, specific, and in the language the family reads at home.

## Why it matters

- **Gives teachers time back.** An hour of daily writing becomes a few minutes of review.
- **Keeps families close.** Consistent, warm updates are what make parents feel involved and confident about their child's day.
- **Reaches every family.** In-language messages include the households a rushed English note leaves behind.

## What it does

- Writes **one update per child** from a single block of shorthand.
- **Tone and length** you control — warm, playful, or straightforward.
- **The family's language** — English, Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, and more.
- **Milestone highlight** that surfaces a real growth moment ("used scissors for the first time").
- **Copy and send** — the teacher always reviews before anything goes out.

It never adds anything that isn't in the notes, and it frames hard moments — a fall, a rough drop-off — honestly but gently.
